## Platform

## Users

Primary users are industrial buyers making their own purchase/rental decision: plant managers, maintenance engineers, and procurement staff at manufacturing and heavy-industry sites (automotive, food & beverage, foundry/die casting, semiconductor/electronics, aerospace, oil & gas, power generation, rail/transit, printing, plastics & composites, mining, rubber & tires, textiles, packaging, engineered wood, medical device manufacturing) who have a cleaning, surface-prep, or contamination problem and are researching dry-ice blasting as a solution. Secondary audience: third-party cleaning-service contractors seeking VATEK-certified partner status, equipment rental, or dry-ice supply to run cleaning jobs for their own clients.

Their job on the site: understand whether dry-ice blasting solves their specific problem, compare it to other cleaning methods, identify the right equipment (blaster/pelletizer/recovery/automation), and move toward a demo test, quote, rental, or purchase.

## Product Purpose

VATEK is the official Korea distributor and service provider for Cold Jet dry-ice cleaning equipment (blasters, pelletizers, CO2 recovery systems, automation integration) and dry-ice supply. The site exists to convert industrial cleaning problems into qualified equipment/rental/demo/service inquiries, and to support the full customer lifecycle from first research through purchase, installation, training, and after-sales support.

## Positioning

VATEK is not just an equipment reseller. As Cold Jet's official Korean distributor, VATEK backs equipment sales with direct, hands-on industrial cleaning experience and supports the entire process domestically: cleaning tests and equipment selection, installation, operator training, after-sales service, rental, certified cleaning-service partners, and custom automation integration. This full-lifecycle, locally-delivered support — combined with sole authorized access to Cold Jet's original patented technology and global Tier-2 technical backup — is the claim a reseller without VATEK's own service track record and Cold Jet backing could not truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- Prospective buyers typically want proof before committing: the site's own copy states "시편에서 안 되는 것은 현장에서도 안 됩니다" (what doesn't work on a test sample won't work in the field either) — demo/sample testing is a normal, expected step before purchase.
- Support model is two-tier: VATEK (Tier 1, based in Hanam, Gyeonggi-do) handles first response and escalates to Cold Jet's global technical team (Tier 2, Ohio, USA) when needed.
- Equipment lifecycle spans: test/demo → quote → purchase or rental → installation/commissioning → operator training → ongoing A/S and inspection → optional Cold Jet CONNECT remote monitoring.
- Site also serves contractors seeking certified-partner status, and supports dry-ice consumable supply (recurring or one-off) independent of equipment purchase.

## Product Principles

- Lead with proof, not claims: every persuasive surface should make it easy to move toward a demo/sample test rather than asking for trust alone.
- Full-lifecycle support is the differentiator, not any single equipment spec sheet — design and content should keep the path from problem → test → equipment → install → ongoing support visible.
- Preserve the dual-brand relationship (VATEK + Cold Jet) as a trust signal; never present VATEK as the sole originator of the underlying technology.
- Speak plainly and confidently to a technical, skeptical industrial buyer — avoid consumer-marketing tone or unverifiable superlatives.
- Never fabricate facts (certifications, addresses, testimonials, benchmarks) where the current source content is explicitly incomplete.
